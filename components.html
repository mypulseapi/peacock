Story Pause & Skip Link

.how-pause

Story pause

Paused

Top-right corner of each How it works card: hidden while the story plays (left), shown while the pointer is on the card and the story is paused (right). A signal only: not a button, not focusable, no hover style. Status text "Paused". JS and motion only.

.skip-link

Skip to content

Skip to content

The first Tab stop on every page (shell:header), visible only while focused, pointing at main#main. Shown here in its focused state.
