It started with one shelf of laptops

We looked at a laptop shelf of 216 products to answer a simple question: if a shopper had to choose, could this catalogue help them? It could not.

The surprise was not the missing data. It was that the usual way of choosing what to fix, emptiest field first, would have spent the effort where it helped shoppers least. That gap between what is missing and what shoppers need is the whole company.

47 of 216

products on the example shelf recorded nothing a shopper could filter on, and 94 could not be told apart on what shoppers need.

Four things we hold to

How we work, and why there is no conversion figure anywhere on this site.

  • Show the working

    The figures on this site are example data, and we say so where they appear. If we cannot show how a figure was produced, we do not print it.

  • Keep the negative results

    We keep the attempt that failed its criteria, the one that passed for the wrong reason, and the one that held.

  • Say what we cannot measure

    Our ranking is steady at the top and less steady below it, and we say so next to the ranking, not in a footnote.

  • Earn the integration

    The first audit needs no code, no tag and no access to your systems. If it is not useful, you have lost an export and an afternoon.

Early, and specific about it

What we can show today, and what we cannot yet.

What we can show

What is proven
The measurement. When we rerun the ranking with different settings, the top of the list stays the same.
What is built
The audit engine, and a live service that re-ranks a catalogue as it changes and explains every score.

What we cannot show yet

What is not proven
Any effect on revenue or conversion. We have run no live experiment, so we have no number and will not offer one.
What we are unsure about
How far the method carries beyond the categories we have measured. We will know when more merchants have run it.
Customers we can name
None yet. The first audit asks for one export and nothing installed, and we will name customers here only with their permission.
