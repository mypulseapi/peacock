Why ecommerce brands should choose PULSE

  1. Capture Shopper Intent from the First Interaction. PULSE reads what a shopper is trying to decide from their very first interaction, before they search, filter or leave.
  2. Connect Shoppers with the Right Products. Each shopper’s intent is matched with the products whose attributes actually answer it, so they reach a suitable product sooner.
  3. Reduce Losing Sales Opportunities from Early Shopper Exits. When products can’t be told apart, shoppers leave early. Fixing the attributes that decide the purchase keeps them choosing.
  4. Turn Commerce Data into Actionable Marketing Intelligence. Your catalogue and shopper signals become a ranked list of what to fix and promote first, not another dashboard to read.
  5. Uncover Insights Others Miss or Fail To Collect. PULSE measures the attributes shoppers need but your catalogue never recorded: the gaps a completeness report cannot see.
