Privacy Policy

At Pulse, privacy isn’t an afterthought – it’s our foundation. We’ve built our technology to deliver powerful personalization without compromising user privacy.

Privacy Highlights

  • No cookies required
  • No PII collection
  • GDPR & CCPA compliant
  • Runs on SOC 2 certified infrastructure

Information We Collect

  • Device type and browser information (non-identifying)
  • Referring website or traffic source
  • General geographic location (country/region level)
  • On-site behavior patterns (searches, filters, browsing)
  • Time of visit and session duration

Note: We do NOT collect: names, emails, addresses, payment information, or any personally identifiable information (PII) without explicit consent.

How We Use Information

  • To provide real-time persona inference for personalized experiences
  • To improve our inference algorithms and accuracy
  • To generate aggregated, anonymized analytics for our clients
  • To maintain and improve our service performance

Data Storage & Security

  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • We run on infrastructure whose providers hold SOC 2 Type II attestations. Pulse itself is not yet SOC 2 audited.
  • Data is stored in secure, geographically distributed data centers
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Automatic data deletion after 90 days unless otherwise specified

What We Don’t Do

  • We don’t use cookies for tracking
  • We don’t sell personal data to third parties
  • We don’t create cross-site tracking profiles
  • We don’t use fingerprinting techniques
  • We don’t share individual user data with advertisers

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Right to access your data
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure (‘right to be forgotten’)
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing

Questions About Privacy?

We’re happy to answer any questions about how we handle your data.
