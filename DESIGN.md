# DESIGN.md — Pulse Design System

Source of truth for how every page and component in this project looks and reads.
Brand colours: **Signal** palette — `docs/pulse_brand_guidelines_final.md` §3–§6 is the source of truth for every value.
Typography: **Geist** + **Geist Mono** — the same guide, §8 (19 Oct 2026, replacing Inter).
Layout, components and voice: originally from the Google Stitch "Pulse Brand Guidelines System".
All tokens live in `src/input.css`.

Live reference: `components.html` shows every component below rendered with the real classes.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Flat and typographic.** Matte, opaque fills. No glassmorphism, no glow, no decorative
   illustration or stock imagery. Depth comes from 1px borders, spacing and `shadow-sm` only, plus one `shadow-window` on the hero demo's app window.
   The only gradients are the Signal gradient (`bg-signal-gradient`), the calm gradient (`bg-calm-gradient`, the How it works progress line), the dark band gradient (`bg-void-gradient`), the soft gradient (`bg-soft-gradient`, the Why Pulse section) and the soft hero background (`hero-bg`, every hero but home's), the home hero's coloured shadow (`hero-glow`), and the aurora behind every hero (`aurora`), see §2.
2. **Data first.** Visual interest comes from tables, ranked lists, scores and status badges —
   not decoration.
3. **Colour has a job.** Every colour below has one meaning. Never use a colour outside its role.
4. **Generous whitespace.** Sections breathe (`py-20`); dense data lives inside cards.
5. **Show, don't tell** (owner, 26 Oct 2026). Keep the words to the minimum a visitor needs and let a
   visual carry the rest: an image, a short video, a diagram, a drawn scene or the product's own UI.
   - A section says one thing: a heading, at most one short paragraph (about 40 words), then the visual.
     A list beats a paragraph; cut any sentence the visual already says.
   - Every section that explains how something works, what changes, or what the merchant gets has a
     visual. A section of prose alone needs a reason.
   - The visual describes the content, never decorates it (principle 1): the product, the data, the flow,
     the before and after. No stock photos: the owner's images, or drawings made from the tokens.
   - Video is muted, loops, needs no sound to follow, and has a poster for reduced motion and no-JS (§6).
   - Words stay where only words work: headings, the lede, alt text, captions, and the limits a buyer
     forwards. Cutting text never cuts a fact the page needs, and a visual follows the no-invented-content
     rule like any copy (real figures, or labelled examples).
   - Both locales: words inside an image cannot be translated, so prefer pictures with no text in them,
     or draw the words in markup.

## 2. Colour tokens — Signal

Every colour is defined once, in the `COLOURS` block of `@theme` in `src/input.css`. The values come from
`docs/pulse_brand_guidelines_final.md` §3–§5 (Signal; it replaces v2 Aurora); change them there first, then here.

1. **Brand palette** — `--brand-blue` (Pulse Blue), `--brand-red` (Signal Red), `--brand-sky` (Sky Blue), `--brand-ink`,
   `--brand-mist`, plus `--gradient-signal`, `--gradient-calm` and `--gradient-signal-fallback`. Not utilities — never use them in HTML.
2. **Roles** — what pages use (`primary`, `ink`, `gap` …). Every text and surface role is an **exact,
   contrast-checked hex** written as `light-dark(<light>, <dark>)` — never `color-mix()` for text, so the
   ratios in the guideline stay true. Only decorative borders and washes use `color-mix()`.
3. **Theme** — light by default; the header switch (`designed-theme-switch.tsx`, a `role="switch"` button,
   26 Oct 2026) sets `<html data-theme="light|dark">` and is remembered. There is no "System" option; a browser
   that stored it from the old menu follows the OS (`color-scheme: light dark` on `:root`) until the switch is
   pressed. The switch's knob slides and its sun turns to a moon in 300ms `ease-out-soft`, and the page changes
   from the switch outward, a circle grown by a view transition (`data-theme-reveal`, 550ms); with reduced motion
   the knob jumps and the page swaps at once. Beside it, the language switch
   (`designed-locale-switch.tsx`, 27 Oct 2026): a toggle "VI [flag] EN", the pill filled with the current language's flag
   (Viet Nam; English is the UK flag), a white knob on its side, the current label in ink. The whole toggle is one link
   to the same page in the other language: the knob slides (200ms) and then the page loads; with reduced motion it loads
   at once. Because every role is
   `light-dark()`, nothing else is needed for dark mode — no `dark:` classes in HTML. Dark surfaces are cool black
   (Night `#0B0D12`, cards `#14171F`), not navy (final guide, 19 Oct 2026). The one exception is what a token cannot switch,
   such as an image: use the `theme-dark:` variant (OS dark unless `data-theme="light"`, or `data-theme="dark"`),
   e.g. the reversed logo.

Rules:

- Use only the token classes below (`bg-primary`, `text-muted`, `border-line` …). Tailwind's default palette
  is switched off (`--color-*: initial`), so `bg-rose-500`, `text-white`, `bg-slate-100` don't exist —
  `pnpm lint:tw` reports them as `[unknownClass]`.
- Never write colour values in HTML or utilities: no hex, no `rgb()`, no `bg-[#…]`. Need a new colour?
  Add a **role-named** token to the `COLOURS` block (light and dark value, contrast-checked), then list it here.
- Name tokens by role, not hue (`primary`, not `indigo`), so names stay true when the palette changes.
- Opacity modifiers on tokens are fine for decoration (`bg-primary/70`), but **not on text** — `text-muted/60`
  or `opacity-60` on text drops it below 4.5:1.
- Check every new text/background pair with the WCAG formula (script, not estimate), in light **and** dark.

## 3. Typography

Three faces: **Mona Sans** for the four title sizes, **Geist** for smaller headings and body, **Geist Mono** for data (Vercel, SIL OFL — `OFL.txt` beside them), chosen on 19 Oct 2026 from a test of three pairings on real Pulse components, in English and Vietnamese (replacing Inter 4.1; final brand guide §8). Self-hosted from `src/assets/fonts/geist/` (no Google Fonts link): variable weight 100–900, one file per subset (Latin, Latin Extended, Vietnamese; the browser loads only what a page uses); `@font-face` in `src/input.css`, Geist Latin preloaded in each page `<head>`. Body tracking −0.011em, headings lighter (550–600) and tighter (−0.01 to −0.022em, line height 1.1–1.35; a little air between heading lines). **Geist Mono only for data:** the values in data tables (every `<table>` sets Geist Mono with tabular figures; its first column, the product names, and the Missing flags stay in Geist) and numeric readouts such as the bit values on the Decision Audit page (`font-mono`). Never for prose, labels or headings. Until the fonts have loaded, "Geist Fallback" (Arial) and "Geist Mono Fallback" (Courier New), scaled to their metrics, show, so lines do not re-wrap. Tracking follows the size: −0.011em for body text (16px), −0.006em at 14px (`text-sm`, `text-data`), 0 at 12px (`text-xs`, `text-caption`), so small text stays open. Both faces have tabular figures (`tnum` in Geist, fixed-width digits in Geist Mono). **Titles (the owner's Option C, set 26 Oct 2026, final 30 Oct 2026; brand guide §8):** the four title sizes (`text-hero`, `text-headline`, `text-display`, `text-title`) set **Mona Sans** (GitHub, SIL OFL), weight 620, semi-expanded (112%, section titles 108%), through `--font-title`, self-hosted from `public/fonts/mona-sans/` with Geist as its fallback. Big numbers that set `font-display` stay Geist.

Rules:

- Numbers, SKUs, prices, percentages and any column of data → add **`tabular-nums`** so digits line up.
- `font-mono` (Geist Mono) only for data, never for prose, labels or headings. Load no other font beyond Mona Sans for the titles above (no Google Fonts link: every face is self-hosted).
- Headlines and titles (`h1`–`h6`) are short, sentence case, and **never end with a full stop**, even when they are a
  statement ("Know which product data to fix first"; decided 19 Oct 2026). A full stop between two sentences in one
  heading stays ("Send one catalogue. Get the findings in 48 hours").

### Sizes — no flexible sizes inline

Responsive and one-off sizes belong in `@theme`, not in class attributes:

- **No arbitrary values** in HTML: no `w-[43.5%]`, `text-[10px]`, `min-w-[720px]`, `border-[1.5px]`.
- **Data widths are fractions of the real numbers**: `w-94/216`, `w-80/162` (Tailwind computes
  `calc(94 / 216 * 100%)`), so a bar is traceable to its source figures and never hand-rounded.
- **No breakpoint font sizes** (`sm:text-5xl lg:text-6xl`). Big headings use the fluid tokens, which scale
  with the viewport via `clamp()` defined once in `@theme`:

| `text-hero`               | 32 → 54px, 620 | The page headline on the home page and every product page (ten, with Promotion Audit) (owner's Version A, 26 Oct 2026): `clamp(2rem, 1.43rem + 2.431vw, 3.375rem)`, 54px from 1280px. ## 4. Layout & spacing — the ruled frame

Pages use an Attio-style **ruled layout**: one centred column (no side lines), with every section
separated by a horizontal hairline.

```html
<section class="border-b border-line">
  <!-- full-width row, hairline below -->
  <div class="frame">
    <!-- centred column: frame = max-w-frame (70rem) + page margin -->
    <div class="py-16 lg:py-24">…</div>
    <!-- no px-*: the frame is the margin -->
  </div>
</section>
```

- Every section (and the header/footer inner row) wraps its content in `frame` so content edges line up.
  Dark bands use `bg-void` (or `bg-void-gradient`) on the section and `frame-dark` inside.
- Split content **inside** the frame with hairlines, not gaps: `grid … md:divide-x md:divide-line`,
  `divide-y divide-line`, or `border-t border-line` between a heading area and its visual.
- Visual areas (mockups, charts) sit on `blueprint-grid bg-surface-1/50`; text areas sit on the plain page.
- Padding: text areas `px-6 py-12 lg:px-12 lg:py-16`; big statements `py-24 lg:py-32`.
- Desktop grid: 12 columns (`lg:grid-cols-12`). Common splits: 3/9 (sticky nav + content), 5/7, 7/5.
- **The canvas outside the page is `card`** (`html` in base): when the page bounces past its top or bottom it shows the
  navbar / footer colour, never a strip of page colour above the sticky header.
- **No sideways scrolling, ever.** `<body class="overflow-x-clip">` on every page (`clip`, not `hidden`, so the
  sticky header keeps working). Content must also fit by itself: no `min-w-*` tables in sideways-scrolling boxes —
  hide less important columns on small screens instead (the hero demo tables show 2 attribute columns below `sm`,
  SKU from `lg`).
- **The header's full nav starts at `xl` (1280px), and at 88rem in Vietnamese**, whose longer row needs 1380px of page;
  below that the compact sheet carries the same links. A longer row in either language moves its own switch point
  (`:lang(vi) [data-nav]` in `globals.css`), never drops items.
- **On a phone, the compact sheet folds and its actions stay put** (owner's version 1, 27 Oct 2026). Each group is one
  `<details>` row (`MenuGroup` in `designed-header.tsx`) with its name as a heading, its count and a chevron; the six share
  `name="mobile-menu-group"`, so the browser keeps one open, Product first. Developers, Pricing and Open source sit under
  them as pills. Sign In, the call to action, the theme switch and the language toggle are pinned below the list.
- **On a phone, the footer is the same content, tighter** (owner's version 2, 27 Oct 2026; below `sm` only): the email and
  the social icons share a row, each link column lays its links in two columns, and the legal links wrap on one line.
- **From `sm`, the footer's four columns are centred** (owner, 28 Oct 2026): the brand column and the three link columns
  each centre their content and sit centred against each other in the row. The company facts strip under them is gone
  (owner, 29 Oct 2026); founded, headquarters and engineering stay on the About page.
- At most one dark (`bg-void`) band per page.
- Inside cards: `p-6` (compact `p-4`/`p-5`), gaps `gap-4`–`gap-6`.
- Radius: `rounded-xl` cards and windows, `rounded-lg` buttons and inner panels, `rounded` chips,
  `rounded-full` badges and bars.
- Mobile first: every grid collapses to one column below `md`/`lg`; sticky side navs are `hidden lg:block`
  and replaced by an `eyebrow` step label (`01 · The problem`) above each block.
- Long headlines get `text-balance`, paragraphs `text-pretty` — no single-word last lines.

## 7. Interaction & accessibility

- Exception (decided 19 Oct 2026, card redrawn after the owner's reference): each About team card is a borderless card (`bg-card rounded-2xl shadow-sm`) with a round portrait (`src/assets/team/<name>.png`, 384px transparent PNG; initials when there is no photo), the name (`h3`), the role and a LinkedIn icon link. On hover or keyboard focus (`has-focus-visible`, not `focus-within`: a clicked LinkedIn link kept focus and held the card open after the visitor came back from the new tab; and `designed-motion.js` drops focus from any new-tab link after a mouse or touch click, since the first key pressed on return would otherwise make it keyboard focus) a half-circle dome (`rounded-t-full`, as wide as the card, a top-to-bottom Pulse Blue wash `bg-linear-to-b from-primary-tint to-primary-line` (the reference's shape, in the primary colour; owner's choice); the one extra gradient allowed beyond §1) rises a quarter of its height and fades in behind the text (500ms; with reduced motion it only fades). Only the profile icon is a link (room for more icons later, e.g. GitHub); its focus outline is `ink` so it stays visible on the dome; in dark mode the dome starts from the card colour (`theme-dark:from-card`) so the blue only builds at its base and the portrait gets a 3px `ink` ring and zooms 20% from its base (500ms, none with reduced motion) while its head steps out over the ring (`team-pop`: a second copy of the photo clipped to its top half, so only the head crosses the frame); the card itself never moves or lifts; it only deepens from `shadow-sm` to `shadow-xl` (owner's request, the one place §9's no-`shadow-lg`+ rule is relaxed).
- Hover: colour/border change only (`hover:bg-primary-hover`, `hover:text-ink`, `hover:border-ink`),
  `transition-colors duration-200 ease-out-soft`. No scale, lift or bounce on hover.
- Focus: 2px `focus` outline with a 2px offset on every interactive element (base `:focus-visible`; components add
  `focus-visible:outline-2 focus-visible:outline-focus`). Never remove outlines.
- Disabled: `disabled` attribute (styles are built in).
- Text contrast ≥ WCAG AA. `muted` on white is the lightest allowed body text.
- Every icon-only button needs `aria-label`; decorative SVGs get `aria-hidden="true"`; generated visuals
  (`data-shelf`) need `role="img"` and an `aria-label` with the real numbers.
- Use semantic elements (`header`, `main`, `section`, `article`, `nav`, `table`, `ol/ul`).

## 8. Voice & tone — "The Honest Data Analyst"

Quietly confident, empirical, calm. Peer to a merchandising lead.

| Do                                                                                             | Don't                                                           |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Concrete numbers and direct cause: "Fixing bottle volume on 42 SKUs unblocks filtered search." | Vague AI magic: "Revolutionary AI supercharges your catalogue!" |
| State confidence and limits: "Confidence 82%. Not enough data beyond rank #4."                 | Unfalsifiable guarantees: "100% infallible."                    |
| Prioritise: "Fix the top 3 attributes first."                                                  | Dump everything with no priority.                               |
| Calm, plain English.                                                                           | Hype, jargon, emoji, exclamation marks.                         |

## 9. Never

- A small label above a heading (`<p class="reveal eyebrow">…</p>` before an `h1`–`h6`): removed from every page on
  19 Oct 2026 at the owner's request. Headings stand alone; never add one back.
- Gradients other than `bg-signal-gradient`, `bg-calm-gradient`, `bg-void-gradient`, `bg-soft-gradient`, `hero-bg` and `aurora`; glassmorphism, neon/glow shadows (exceptions: `hero-glow` behind the home hero demo, and the `shadow-glow` halo on the Why Pulse centre icon and selected circle), `shadow-lg`+ on light cards.
- Decorative looping animation (spinners, floating blobs, parallax), except the hero `aurora` the owner asked for; hover scale/lift effects.
- `signal` (Signal Red) more than once per view (two at most); red buttons; `gap` (amber) for anything that isn't missing data; `critical` for missing data or priority.
- Telling the two reds apart by colour alone: an error (`critical`) always carries the error icon (`#i-triangle-alert`) and a word; the #1 (`signal` / `signal-text`) always carries the priority ring and its rank number.
- Small or body text on the Signal gradient (white only, large text only); Signal Red, Sky or amber as text on light backgrounds (use `signal-text`, `link`, `gap-strong`); opacity reductions on text; Violet or Cyan decoration (Violet exists only inside the gradient).
- Colours outside the theme tokens; arbitrary `[…]` values or breakpoint font sizes in HTML (see §2, §3).
- Raw hex values or default Tailwind colours where a token exists.
- Monospace outside data (Geist Mono is for table values and numeric readouts only), extra font families, fonts or icon fonts loaded from a CDN.
- Inline `style=""` or new `<style>` blocks in HTML — add to `src/input.css` instead.
