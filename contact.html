Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the first audit take?

Forty-eight hours from the moment your export lands. There is nothing to integrate first, because the audit reads a file rather than your website. Most teams get the export out of their PIM or platform in under an hour.

Is the first audit really free?

Yes. One category, one export, no account and no card. We would rather you judged the method on your own products than on somebody else's case study. What comes after is priced per cycle, and nobody has to decide that on day one.

How quickly will someone reply?

Usually the same working day. The inbox is read by the people who do the work rather than by a queue, so the reply comes from someone who can actually open your catalogue.

Can an agency run this for a client?

We have no partner programme to point you at yet, and we would rather say so than invent one. We do run audits for agencies on behalf of their clients, and it takes the same single export either way. Write to the address above and tell us whose catalogue it is.

Would you rather see it live?

We walk through an audit on a real catalogue, answer the awkward questions, and show you where the method stops.
