The ones that decide it

What exactly do I need to send you?

One category export as CSV or JSON, containing product name, description, category, price and any attributes you already record. Nothing else. No customer records, no order history, no access to your systems, and no code on your site. Most merchants can produce this from their PIM or platform in under an hour.

Do I need a technical coordinator to use the product?

No. One person to produce the export, and whoever owns merchandising for the walkthrough. No engineering time, because nothing is being installed.

How long does it take to receive the result?

48 hours from receiving the file. The measurement itself is fast; the time is in reading your taxonomy properly so the attributes we score are the ones your category actually uses.
